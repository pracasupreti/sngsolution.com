Background

The global banking industry is undergoing a foundational shift driven by Artificial Intelligence and intelligent automation. Agentic AI now marks the next leap, where autonomous systems understand goals, coordinate workflows, retrieve information, and execute decisions with minimal human intervention. For Nepali banks, this is no longer a distant trend, it is a near-term competitive imperative. NIC ASIA Bank, as one of the country's largest commercial banks, stands at the inflection point where early adoption of agentic systems can deliver measurable advantages in cost efficiency, customer experience, compliance, and operational resilience. This in-house workshop is purpose built to give executive and senior management teams a strategic, practical, and demonstrably real exposure to Agentic AI applied to banking workflows.

Objectives
  • Introduce Agentic AI and its rapid evolution from rule based to autonomous systems
  • Clearly differentiate General AI, Generative AI, and Agentic AI in a banking context
  • Explore sector specific applications across service, credit, compliance, and operations
  • Provide live exposure to intelligent workflow automation platforms
  • Demonstrate real, banking relevant AI use cases through hands-on walkthroughs
  • Promote responsible, secure, and governance-aware AI adoption
Key Takeaways
  • Conceptual clarity on Agentic AI versus Generative and General AI
  • Practical exposure to n8n, Microsoft Copilot Studio, and AI agent frameworks
  • Sector relevant use cases for customer support, loan processing, KYC, and reporting
  • Templates and reference workflows that can be piloted internally
  • Strategic readiness checklist for institutional AI adoption
  • Certificate of participation jointly issued by SNG Solution and the facilitator
Value Added for NIC ASIA
  • Immediate productivity gains in repetitive operational workflows
  • Reduced manual workload across mid-office and back-office functions
  • Executive readiness to evaluate AI vendor proposals
  • Foundation for an internal Agentic AI playbook and pilot roadmap

Session 1

1Introduction to the AI Evolution

A structured walkthrough of how artificial intelligence has matured from rule based and statistical systems to deep learning, then to Generative AI, and now to Agentic AI. Participants gain a clear mental model of where each generation of AI fits in banking, what each can and cannot do, and why this distinction matters for strategy, vendor evaluation, and risk governance.

History of AIGenerative AIAgentic AIBanking lens
Session 2

2General AI vs Agentic AI

A focused comparison of conventional general-purpose AI assistants and goal-driven agentic systems. The session unpacks how agentic AI plans, decides, calls tools, and chains tasks autonomously, and explores where this autonomy creates value in banking versus where human-in-the-loop must remain.

Tool usePlanningAutonomyHuman-in-loop
Session 3

3Fundamentals of Agentic AI

The technical and operational anatomy of an AI agent, including reasoning loops, memory, tool integration, retrieval augmented generation, and multi-agent coordination. Concepts are explained in plain language with banking-relevant analogies so that non-technical executives can confidently participate in strategy and architecture conversations.

AgentsMemoryRAGOrchestration
Session 4

4Applications in Banking

A deep dive into where agentic AI is already creating measurable value in banks globally, including intelligent customer service, KYC and onboarding, AML monitoring, loan documentation review, branch operations, internal knowledge assistants, and finance reporting. Each application is mapped to typical NIC ASIA workflow categories.

Customer serviceKYC & AMLCredit opsReporting

5Executive Discussion & Institutional Readiness

A facilitated executive dialogue on opportunities, constraints, regulatory considerations, vendor and build-versus-buy choices, and what institutional readiness looks like. Demo 1

1AI Workflow Automation using n8n

A live build-along of agentic workflows in n8n, an open source automation platform widely adopted by financial institutions for orchestrating intelligent processes. Participants observe how approval routing, automated MIS reporting, email triage, and operational coordination tasks can be designed, deployed, and monitored without heavy custom engineering.

ApprovalsMISEmail triageOperations
Demo 2

2Microsoft Copilot Studio for Internal Banking Assistants

A practical demonstration of building a custom enterprise AI assistant on Microsoft Copilot Studio, tuned to internal banking policies, product manuals, and operational SOPs. Participants see how such an assistant can serve as a first line of support for branch staff, reducing reliance on phone escalations and accelerating internal knowledge retrieval.

Copilot StudioInternal AIBranch enablementSOP automation
Demo 3

3Loan & Risk Analysis with AI Agents

A walkthrough of how an AI agent can read loan files, extract key data, cross-check against credit policy, flag inconsistencies, and prepare a structured pre-credit memo for human credit officers. The session also explores how similar agents support risk assessment, early warning signals, and portfolio monitoring while keeping human judgement firmly in control.

Loan filesCredit memoRisk monitoringEarly warnings
Demo 4

4Social Media & Communication Automation

A demonstration of how marketing and communication teams can use AI agents to plan campaigns, draft compliant social posts, route customer queries from public channels, and produce performance reports. The session emphasises brand safety, regulatory disclosure norms, and reputational risk management in a regulated financial institution.

CampaignsBrand safetyCustomer voiceReporting
